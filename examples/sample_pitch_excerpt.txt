Problem: Small e-commerce brands lose 15-20% of revenue to cart abandonment, and most
existing recovery tools rely on generic email sequences that convert poorly because
they ignore why a specific customer hesitated.

Solution: We built a checkout-recovery product that infers the likely reason for
abandonment from on-site behavior (price hesitation, shipping cost surprise, payment
friction) and sends a targeted follow-up matched to that reason instead of a one-size
email. In our first 6 months with 40 pilot merchants, recovered-cart revenue increased
by an average of 22% over their previous recovery tooling.

We are raising a seed round to expand the behavioral model to more verticals and
build out a self-serve onboarding flow.
